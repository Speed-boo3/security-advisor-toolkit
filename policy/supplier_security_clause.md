# Supplier Security Clause Library

Standard clauses for insertion into supplier contracts and agreements.
Based on ISO 27001:2022 Annex A 5.19-5.22 and NIS2 requirements.

---

## Clause 1 -- Information security requirements

The supplier shall maintain an information security management system appropriate
to the nature and sensitivity of data and systems accessed. Where the supplier
holds ISO 27001 certification or equivalent, a copy of the current certificate
shall be provided on request.

---

## Clause 2 -- Data protection and confidentiality

The supplier shall process organisation data only for the purposes documented
in this agreement. Personal data shall be handled in accordance with applicable
data protection legislation including GDPR. The supplier shall not share
organisation data with subcontractors without prior written consent.

---

## Clause 3 -- Incident notification

The supplier shall notify the organisation of any confirmed or suspected security
incident affecting organisation data or systems within 24 hours of discovery.
Notification shall include the nature of the incident, data affected, and
immediate containment actions taken.

---

## Clause 4 -- Right to audit

The organisation reserves the right to audit the supplier's security controls
relevant to this agreement, either directly or via an appointed third party,
with reasonable notice. The supplier shall cooperate fully with such audits.

---

## Clause 5 -- Personnel security

The supplier shall ensure that personnel with access to organisation data or
systems have undergone appropriate background checks and completed security
awareness training relevant to their role.

---

## Clause 6 -- Subcontractors

The supplier shall not engage subcontractors in the delivery of this agreement
without prior written consent from the organisation. Where subcontractors are
approved, equivalent security requirements shall apply.

---

## Clause 7 -- Return and deletion of data

Upon termination of this agreement, the supplier shall return or securely
delete all organisation data within 30 days and provide written confirmation
that deletion has been completed.
