# Information Security Policy Template

Version: 1.0
Date: September 2026
Review cycle: Annual

Built around ISO 27001:2022, NIST CSF 2.0 and NSM Grunnprinsipper.
NIS2 requirements are marked where relevant.

---

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets out the minimum requirements for protecting information assets.
It applies to all employees, contractors and suppliers who access our systems or data.

---

## 2. Roles

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Management | Accountability for security risk (NIS2) |
| Security advisor | Policy ownership, risk oversight, audit support |
| IT | Control implementation, patch management, monitoring |
| All staff | Follow policy, complete training, report incidents |
| Suppliers | Comply with contractual security requirements |

---

## 3. Risk management

Risks are identified, scored and treated following a structured process.
Risk register is reviewed quarterly. Critical risks require immediate management decision.

---

## 4. Access control

- Least privilege applies to all accounts
- MFA required for all remote and privileged access
- Access reviewed every 90 days
- Accounts disabled within 24 hours of a role change

---

## 5. Supplier security

- Security assessment completed before onboarding any supplier with data access
- Contracts include security requirements and breach notification obligations
- Critical suppliers reviewed annually
- See supplier_checklist.md for the assessment process

---

## 6. Incidents (NIS2)

- Incidents reported to Security Advisor within 1 hour
- NIS2 early warning to authority within 24 hours
- Full notification within 72 hours
- Post-incident report within 5 working days

---

## 7. AI and new technology

- Approval required before using AI tools for work purposes
- Classified or personal data must not be submitted to external AI services
- Aligned with ISO/IEC 42001:2023

---

## 8. Exceptions

All exceptions require written approval and are tracked in the risk register.
Maximum duration 90 days.
